 Project 01 — Risk Register & Heat Map

**Company:** Shibu Cloud Solutions Pvt Ltd  
**Framework:** ISO 27001:2022 — Clause 6.1 (Risk Assessment)  
**Tool:** Google Sheets  
**Status:** ✅ Complete

## What I Built
- 10-risk register covering Cybersecurity, Compliance, 
  Operational, Third-party, and Physical risk categories
- 5x5 qualitative risk scoring matrix (Likelihood × Impact)
- Color-coded risk heat map with all risks plotted
- Risk owners, treatment strategies, and mitigation actions
- Review dates aligned to risk severity

## Key Findings
- 4 Critical risks identified (Score 15–25)
- 4 High risks identified (Score 10–14)  
- 2 Medium risks identified (Score 5–9)
- Top risk: Phishing attack on employee credentials (Score 20)
